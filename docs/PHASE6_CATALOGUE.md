# Skytrace Phase 6 capability catalogue

Generated by `python scripts/phase6_catalogue.py`; every count is computed from the rows in that script. Checked by `tests/unit/test_phase6_catalogue.py`: well formed, every cited file and test function exists, every concept marked existing or new cites evidence and a verification, and synonyms are merged rather than counted.

The 300 concepts and 30 domains are those of the Phase 6 brief (§19), named as the brief names them. Where two brief concepts name the same capability, the second is recorded with *duplicate of* and not counted; concepts this project added are listed at the end and counted. The Phase 5 catalogue (`docs/FEATURE_CATALOGUE.md`) is kept unchanged.

**309 distinct concepts** (300 from the brief − 15 merged synonyms + 24 added), in 30 domains. Status: 97 existing, 142 new in 0.6, 64 deferred, 1 blocked, 5 out of scope. Groups: 104 Foundation, 137 High Impact, 36 Signature, 32 Frontier.

Status: **existing** built in 0.5 or earlier · **new in 0.6** built in this release · **deferred** not built, nothing claims it · **blocked** needs a service this environment refuses · **out of scope** not applicable or not advisable. Groups follow §20: Foundation (P1), High Impact and Signature (P2), Frontier (P3). A priority is a planning signal; the status says what is built.
