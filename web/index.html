Static preview · results precomputed by the Skytrace pipeline; nothing is contacted while you browse. What this means

Every result here was produced by the Skytrace server and Python pipeline when this preview was built, from the bundled synthetic test targets. While you browse, no server, archive or language model is contacted. Re-runs offer the precomputed parameter sets (defaults and the five stability perturbations); pair comparisons and image registration are computed in your browser from the record; anything that needs the server says so. Run python run.py for the full application.

Observatory Command Center

The state of this research session at a glance: target, question, coverage, data quality, analysis, explanations, evidence gaps, recent activity and what to do next. Every value comes from the stored session or the recorded analysis.

Adaptive Investigation Planner

Turn a research question into a plan of registered analyses: what evidence exists, which steps can run, what blocks the others, and what each result says. Steps that re-run analyses wait for your confirmation; nothing is simulated.

Scientific Experiment Studio

Define bounded experiments — parameter, sensitivity, reproducibility, method comparison, negative control, robustness — with stated criteria, and automate multi-step workflows. An experiment that ran is not, by itself, a valid experiment.

Data Quality Observatory

Named checks with explicit outcomes — valid under the tested rules, invalid, incompatible, not available, not checked — the observation compatibility matrix, metadata, coverage, and the archive connectors. There is no overall quality score.

Discovery Radar

Research opportunities: what might be worth examining next, why, on what evidence, with which method, and what it cannot establish. These are recommendations, not detections.

Research Notebook

Structured notes kept with the session: text you write, and blocks that reference observations, measurements, claims, explanations, experiments and figures by identifier. Referenced values are read from the record, never typed; your interpretations stay labelled as yours.

Scientific Intelligence

What this investigation knows, what it does not yet know, why, and what evidence could change that — in six connected layers sharing one focus. Observation (the command center and the discovery genome), Discovery (the unknown universe and the relationships between investigations), Reasoning (the scientific adversary and the causality lab), Experimental (what-if questions, observation planning and the bounded research cycle), Knowledge (research memory and the reproduction network) and Integrity (the gate an investigation passes before it is marked complete). Every item names its rule and where it is recorded; real data, synthetic data, simulated outputs, hypothetical scenarios, unverified interpretations and verified computations are labelled apart.

Reasoning Graph

How each conclusion is constructed: source files, observations, measurements, derived quantities, inferences and claims, with the transformations, methods, models, assumptions and uncertainty that produced them. Every object carries an epistemic status assigned by stated rules from its origin and method — never from how a sentence sounds. Trace a claim back to its files, or a file forward to everything that depends on it; Lineage-X follows a result through every recorded transformation.

Negative Space

What was attempted and could not be established: excluded images, refused measurements, invalid comparisons, tests not run or inconclusive, unavailable services, unresolved contradictions, failed reproductions and hypotheses you abandoned. Each entry says why it was attempted, what happened, what remains unknown and what would unblock it. Failed work is evidence too, and nothing here is erased.

Model Arena

Competing models fitted to the same recorded visit positions — a static position and linear motion — compared with standard criteria for Gaussian errors (χ², degrees of freedom, Δχ², AIC), residuals and leave-one-visit-out refits. The better-fitting model describes these points better; it is not thereby the physical explanation.

Uncertainty Flow

How uncertainty travels from per-pixel variance to the stated sensitivities, transformation by transformation: what each step propagates, how, under which assumptions, with which recorded result — and which effects no σ includes.

Scientific Debugger

Why was this output produced? Breakpoints at the execution stages the record actually contains; for a selected result, the exact computation, its parameters and recorded inputs, and a recomputation where the record determines it. Compare two runs to find the earliest stage where they diverge, or re-execute a run to see how closely it reproduces.

Scientific Compiler

Structural and methodological checks on the whole investigation, in ten stages — parse, types and units, dependencies, assumptions, method preconditions, evidence, uncertainty, provenance, conclusions, report — each check with its rule and the objects it cites. A successful compilation means the investigation passed these checks, not that its conclusions are true. The method genome documents every method as a versioned object.

Claims & Blindspots

Where each claim stands — established by the recorded evidence, supported under stated assumptions, unresolved, or outside the evidence — and why, by stated rules rather than confidence numbers. Stress-test a claim in ten modes, and see which parts of the investigation have not been examined against a defined list of checks.

FutureLab

How could future measurements change this investigation? Hypothetical scenarios — a longer baseline, more visits, better precision, missing metadata supplied — computed with formulas that first reproduce the recorded fit. Every scenario states its assumptions; nothing here is a scheduled observation.

Questions & Next Steps

What to investigate next, as transparent decision records — the action, the reasoning, the evidence used, what it needs, its limitations and why other actions were placed lower — and research questions that arise from this investigation's evidence and gaps, each citing what prompted it.

Protocol & Blind Analysis

Write down the question, hypotheses, inputs, methods, exclusions, parameters, evaluation criteria and robustness plans before analysing; lock them; amend only with a reason. Optionally hide outcomes while you work and unblind through a documented step. This separates planned choices from choices made after seeing results.

Reasoning Lab

Construct an inspectable scientific argument from the recorded evidence — claim, supporting and contradicting evidence, assumptions, inference rule, limitations, alternatives, open questions — branch it, compare it, challenge its steps and export the map. Logical validity and empirical truth are judged separately.

Integrity Firewall

Check a report, your notebook or any text against the recorded investigation before you share it. Advice, never a block: every warning can be inspected, and the text is never changed.

Discovery Horizon

What scientific work remains possible with the evidence available: the investigation laid out in six layers by the Scientific Possibility Engine, then research opportunities — each with its question, the evidence that prompted it, the data it needs, the method, its limits, feasibility and why it is ranked where it is. Opportunities, never findings.

Counterfactual Universe Lab

Change one stated thing — a parameter, an excluded input, a threshold, the error model, the method — and see exactly which results change. The original investigation is never altered, and a scenario is not an observation or an alternative physical reality.

Synthetic Observatory

Generate labelled synthetic scenarios with known ground truth, run the unchanged pipeline on them, and compare expected, measured and algorithm output — and compare hypothetical observation schedules. Generated data test the software; they never replace observations.

Adversarial Science Lab

Challenge the investigation's own interpretation with documented rules — competing explanations, method assumptions, evidence weaknesses, sensitivity, blind spots — rank the open tests, and separate dependency, assumption and proposal in the causality workbench. A challenge is something to examine, not an established flaw.

Research Autopilot

A controlled orchestrator: state an objective, review the proposed workflow of registered actions, choose a permission level and authorize; each step is validated, executed, recorded and stopped on failure. Network access is never granted here.

Cosmic Atlas

Where the targets are on the sky and how far apart they appear. Angular separations are computed; distances are shown only when a parallax is recorded; nothing three-dimensional is inferred from sky positions.

Knowledge Atlas

Search everything this installation holds — investigations, sessions, notes, hypotheses, opportunities, challenges, methods, concepts — with exact, structured matching, and compare Astro-Twins: hashed records of analyses, targets and investigations.

Review Room & Discovery Story

Structured software review by five rule sets, the Reproducibility Challenge with its stated verdict rule, and a story of the investigation written only from recorded outcomes. The reviewers are rule sets, not people.

Discovery Engine

What this investigation can establish, what it cannot, and what to do next: evidence inventory, research questions with test plans, review, and next steps, all derived from the recorded analysis.

Sky Explorer

Inspect every archive image that covers the target, with its date, detector, wavelength and provenance.

Time Machine

Compare two observations. Brightness can only be compared at a matched wavelength; positions can be compared across wavelengths, with care.

Discovery Lab

What the measurements support, how they were made, and what else could explain them.

Hypothesis Arena

Competing explanations, each tested against recorded values by a stated rule. No probabilities: a test is consistent, contradicted, inconclusive, or says why it could not be run.

Evidence Ledger

Every node is a recorded fact (an observation, a measurement, a method, a comparison, a claim or a limitation) or a competing explanation with the results of its recorded tests. Select a claim to trace it back to its observations.

Vault & Reports

The investigation vault and report studio: save an investigation with its parameters, data checksums, results, research question, your hypotheses and the copilot conversation; search, tag, compare, reopen or replay it; export the report, record, evidence graph or a reproducibility package.

Research copilot

Run an analysis to ask questions about its evidence.
